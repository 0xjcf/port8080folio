# Editorial article cover prompts

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool. Each illustration is a separate generation, with no input images. The screenshots supplied by the user are evidence of the reader UI, not image-generation inputs.

Production copies are JPEG encodings of the selected generated artwork, retaining the original composition. Cover art belongs in feed and social previews; article reading layouts remain unchanged.
